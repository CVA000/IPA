Vowel Field

Field Notes on Variation

No two speakers share an identical inventory. These are the processes you will actually hear, and each one decides whether something is a disorder or a dialect.

05

Diacritics & Allophones

Slash marks / / hold a mental category, a phoneme. Square marks [ ] hold what actually came out of the mouth, a phone. These are the marks that record the difference.

PhonoBook

An interactive guide to the General American English phoneme inventory, built as a study companion for speech-language pathology students. Transcription conventions follow standard North American clinical practice. The vocal tract model is procedural, built from published articulatory data, and animates the tongue across the vowel space.
